03 · PROTECTIVE CORE MATERIALS

Three Lead-Free Cores. One Standard of Safety.

Inspired by leading global shielding science, GulfRay offers three verified lead-free attenuation cores. All three are 100% toxic-lead-free, compliant with international standards, and specified in true mm lead equivalence (Pb-eq).

UM · Ultra-Lightest

UM Core

About 28% lighter than traditional lead
  • Areal Density: 2.60 kg/m²
  • Core Thickness: 0.66 mm
  • Standard Tested: ASTM F2547-18
  • Tube Voltage: 60–110 kV
Best Clinical Use: Demanding, extended cardiac catheterization, interventional radiology, and long orthopaedic surgeries where every gram counts.
NPL · Broad-Beam UK Verified

NPL Core

Broad-beam gold-standard geometry
  • Areal Density: 2.83 kg/m²
  • Core Thickness: 0.71 mm
  • Standard Tested: BS EN 61331-1:2014
  • Testing Body: UK National Physical Lab
Best Clinical Use: Comprehensive hospital departments requiring rigorous broad-beam test certificates under international IEC guidelines.
ML · Most Economical

ML Core

Accessible price for larger departments
  • Areal Density: 3.60 kg/m²
  • Core Thickness: 0.96 mm
  • Standard Tested: GBZ/T 147-2020
  • Tube Voltage: 60–110 kV
Best Clinical Use: General radiology, outpatient scanning suites, and public hospital tenders seeking premier lead-free protection within budget constraints.
